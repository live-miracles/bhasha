# Listener Mobile Checklist

Run this checklist before using a program for a live production event.

## iPhone Safari

- Open `/{programSlug}` from a QR scan and from a direct link.
- Confirm the page loads without any microphone or camera prompt.
- Tap a live language and confirm audio starts only after the tap.
- Switch to another language and confirm the previous stream stops.
- Lock/unlock the phone, return to Safari, and use the visible reconnect control if playback drops.
- Leave the stream and confirm active listener counts fall after the next status refresh.

## Android Chrome

- Open `/{programSlug}` from a QR scan and from a direct link.
- Confirm the page loads without any microphone or camera prompt.
- Tap a live language and confirm audio starts only after the tap.
- Switch to another language and confirm the previous stream stops.
- Background/foreground Chrome, then use the visible reconnect control if playback drops.
- Leave the stream and confirm active listener counts fall after the next status refresh.

## Notes To Capture

- Device model and OS/browser version.
- Program slug and language stream tested.
- Whether audio started on the first tap.
- Any reconnect, switch, or leave failures.
- Whether status/count updates looked stale or degraded.
